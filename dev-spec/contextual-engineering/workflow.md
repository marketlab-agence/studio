# Contextual Engineering Workflow

This document outlines a typical workflow for applying Contextual Engineering principles in your software development process.

1.  **Define the Goal:** Clearly articulate the objective of the task you want to accomplish with AI assistance.

2.  **Write the Specification:** Create a detailed specification for the AI agent, outlining the input, output, constraints, and providing examples.

3.  **Select and Configure Agents:** Choose the appropriate AI agent(s) for the task and configure them with the necessary parameters.

4.  **Utilize Agent Hooks:** Integrate agent hooks into your workflow to trigger AI assistance at relevant stages (e.g., code generation, testing).

5.  **Provide Context:** Ensure the AI agent has access to the necessary context, such as the codebase, relevant documentation, and project requirements.

6.  **Initiate and Monitor:** Start the AI agent and monitor its progress and output.

7.  **Steer and Refine:** Actively steer the agent by providing feedback, adjusting the specification, and intervening if necessary.

8.  **Review and Integrate:** Review the agent's output and integrate it into your codebase or workflow.

9.  **Iterate:** Repeat the process as needed, refining the specification, agent configuration, and steering techniques based on the results.

10. **Manage with MCP Server:** (For larger projects) Utilize an MCP server to manage and orchestrate multiple agents, monitor performance, and enforce security.

This workflow emphasizes a structured and iterative approach to AI-assisted development, enabling developers to leverage AI effectively while maintaining control and ensuring quality.
